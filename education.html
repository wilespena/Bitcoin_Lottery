🏆 You found the key — now what?

Read this carefully before doing anything. One wrong move could cost you everything.

⚠️ Warning

Claiming is NOT as simple as sending a transaction

The moment you broadcast a transaction from the puzzle address, you expose its public key to the entire network. Automated bots monitor the mempool 24/7 looking for exactly this. They can instantly submit a competing transaction with a higher fee, stealing your Bitcoin before your transaction confirms.

Disabling RBF (Replace-By-Fee) does NOT protect you. Bots can still use other fee-bumping strategies. The public key exposure is the real vulnerability.

🦘 Why is the public key a problem?

Bitcoin puzzle addresses are generated from a known private key range. Researchers and bots have already computed the public keys for many of these addresses using algorithms like Pollard's Kangaroo. Once your transaction hits the mempool, they know the public key and can craft a conflicting transaction faster than yours confirms.

✅ Recommended Method

Safe withdrawal via MARA Slipstream

MARA Slipstream is a private transaction submission service by Marathon Digital Holdings, one of the largest Bitcoin mining pools. When you submit a transaction through Slipstream, it is never broadcast to the public mempool. MARA mines it directly into a block silently — your public key is only revealed after the block is confirmed. By then, it's too late for bots to do anything.

📋 Step-by-step: How to claim safely

1
Do NOT broadcast anything yet.
Close any wallet that might auto-broadcast. Do not import the private key into a standard wallet connected to the internet.
2
Build your transaction offline.
Use an air-gapped computer or an offline signing tool to create and sign a transaction sending the BTC to a new, fresh wallet address you control. Do not connect to the internet during this step.
3
Submit ONLY to MARA Slipstream.
Copy your signed raw transaction (hex) and go to slipstream.mara.com. Submit the transaction there. It will be added privately to MARA's next block without touching the public mempool.
4
Wait for confirmation.
Once MARA mines a block containing your transaction, it will appear on the blockchain confirmed. Only at this point is your public key revealed — but the transaction is already final.
5
Move your funds again.
After the first confirmation, send the BTC from the new address to your final secure cold wallet. From here, normal transactions are safe.
💡 Tip

MARA Slipstream is free to use. You just need to set a competitive fee rate so MARA is incentivized to include your transaction. Check current fee rates at mempool.space before building your transaction.

🔍 How does this lottery work?

This app runs 100% in your browser — no server, no API calls, no data transmitted anywhere. Every key generated stays on your device.

In normal mode, each roll picks a cryptographically random starting point within the selected puzzle's range and tests the next 50,000 private keys sequentially. In infinite mode, each batch of 1,000 keys is generated completely at random — pure luck, no sequence. Either way, finding the key is statistically improbable, but mathematically possible. Someone will find it eventually.
