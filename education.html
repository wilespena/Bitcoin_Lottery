🧩 Background

What is the Bitcoin Puzzle?

In 2015, an anonymous individual sent Bitcoin to 256 different wallet addresses, each one containing a private key that falls within a progressively larger range of difficulty. The amounts grow with the puzzle number — puzzle #1 has 0.001 BTC, puzzle #71 has 7.1 BTC, and so on up to puzzle #256.

The challenge was designed to test the limits of brute-force key searching and to reward anyone clever (or lucky) enough to find the private key. The ranges are known — the puzzle creator published them — but the search space is astronomically large. Puzzle #71 alone has over 2.3 quintillion possible keys.

Some lower-numbered puzzles have already been solved. The higher ones remain open. All funds are sitting untouched on the blockchain, waiting for whoever finds the key first.

📖 Full puzzle list and status: privatekeys.pw/puzzles/bitcoin-puzzle-tx

⚠️ Warning

Claiming is NOT as simple as sending a transaction

The moment you broadcast a transaction from the puzzle address, you expose its public key to the entire network. Automated bots monitor the mempool 24/7 looking for exactly this. They can instantly submit a competing transaction with a higher fee, stealing your Bitcoin before your transaction confirms.

Disabling RBF (Replace-By-Fee) does NOT protect you. Bots can still use other fee-bumping strategies. The public key exposure is the real vulnerability.

🦘 Why is the public key a problem?

Bitcoin puzzle addresses are generated from a known private key range. Researchers and bots have already computed the public keys for many of these addresses using algorithms like Pollard's Kangaroo. Once your transaction hits the mempool, they know the public key and can craft a conflicting transaction faster than yours confirms.

✅ Recommended Method

Safe withdrawal via MARA Slipstream

MARA Slipstream is a private transaction submission service by Marathon Digital Holdings, one of the largest Bitcoin mining pools. When you submit a transaction through Slipstream, it is never broadcast to the public mempool. MARA mines it directly into a block silently — your public key is only revealed after the block is confirmed. By then, it's too late for bots to do anything.

📋 Step-by-step: How to claim safely

1
Do NOT broadcast anything yet.
Close any wallet that might auto-broadcast. Do not import the private key into a standard wallet connected to the internet.
2
Use the built-in Transaction Builder.
When you find the key, a green button "⚡ Build Transaction for MARA Slipstream" appears automatically on the lottery screen. Click it to open the Transaction Builder — it will pre-fill your private key. The tool fetches your UTXO data directly from mempool.space and signs the transaction 100% in your browser — no server, no key ever leaves your device. You do NOT need Electrum or any other wallet.
3
Set a competitive fee rate.
The builder automatically fetches current fee rates from mempool.space and calculates the MARA Slipstream minimum for you: max(2 × priority fee, 2 sat/vByte). Three presets are shown — Normal ⛏ (MARA min), Priority ⚡ (2×) and Fast 🚀 (3×). Choose at least the MARA minimum so your transaction gets included.
4
Copy the Payload and submit it on MARA Slipstream.
The TxBuilder does NOT submit the transaction automatically (to avoid browser CORS restrictions). Instead, it produces a clean signed HEX payload. Click "Copy" or use the link to open the official MARA Slipstream portal in a new tab. Paste the code there and click Submit.
5
Move your funds to cold storage.
MARA will show you the block status on their site. After the first confirmation on the blockchain, immediately send the BTC from the intermediate address you provided in the builder to your final secure cold wallet (Hardware wallet). From here, any normal transaction is 100% safe.
💡 How the fee is auto-calculated

The Transaction Builder calls mempool.space/api/v1/fees/recommended on load. MARA's documented minimum is the higher of 2× current priority fee rate or 2 sat/vByte. The builder uses this formula to freeze the exact sat fee and builds the Raw Transaction with the precision required by institutional mining pools. (Never decrease the fee input — only increase if you need to compete during peak mempool moments.)

🔍 How does this lottery work?

This app runs 100% in your browser — no server, no API calls, no data transmitted anywhere. Every key generated stays on your device.

There are 3 search modes available:

🎲
Manual
Each click picks a cryptographically random starting point within the selected puzzle's range and tests the next 50,000 private keys sequentially. One roll at a time — you control the pace.
🎰
∞ Random
Runs an endless loop testing batches of 1,000 completely random keys per cycle — each key is independently generated via window.crypto. No sequence, pure luck. Runs until you click Stop or the key is found.
🔁
∞ Sequential
Runs an endless loop where each batch picks a new random starting point and then tests N keys sequentially from there (default 50,000, fully customizable — minimum 50k). Combines the coverage guarantee of sequential search with the unpredictability of a random starting point. Runs until you click Stop or the key is found.
